How Are We Doing
New commissioners gave the agency high marks in explaining the application and appointment process, including timliness of onboarding, communication with the agency.
Factors Affecting Results
Some commissioners struggle to understand their role and what is expected of them. They report the satisfaction of their experience as a commissioner meeting their expectations as 4 out of 5.  New
commissioners expressed frustration with their respective commission leaders in connecting with them.  This does not relflect directly on the agency, however the agency could encourage leadership
to reach out which would be under the purvue of us.   There is room for the agency to improve.
