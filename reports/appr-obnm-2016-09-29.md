How Are We Doing
Not good, not good at all.  the number of cases has not increased greatly, but the complexity and in-depth investigation required has increased.
Factors Affecting Results
investigations are requiring more time. OBNM only has a .5FTE investigator.
2014 cases taking more than 6 month averaged 10.75 months for each of the 7 cases; cases in 2015 (4 are still being investigated) are averaging 11.50 for each of the  8 cases.
a 1.0FTE investigator would help the OBNM achieve the desired goal of 6 months per complaint. (the OBNM has submitted a policy package for the 2017-19 budget to increase this position)
