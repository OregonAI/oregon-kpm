How Are We Doing
LC's goal is to provide premium legal and publication services to the Legislative Assembly. In August 2025, a customer satisfaction survey was conducted to rate the overall service of the agency. The
survey was sent to Legislators and legislative staff. There were 69 respondents who rated the Legislative Counsel section of the survey. The percentage of respondents who rated Customer Service
as "Good" or "Excellent" for Timeliness - 97%; Accuracy - 96%; Helpfulness - 99%; Expertise - 100%; Overall Quality of Services - 100%. Ninety-seven percent of respondents answered that the
Quality of Services Provided by Legislative Counsel were "Getting Better" or "Staying the Same".
Factors Affecting Results
Legislative Counsel employees strive to provide excellent client service and perform Legislative Counsel functions in a timely fashion with accuracy and expertise, while always abiding with our status
as a nonpartisan office.  As our actuals exceed targets in this KPM, we intend to continue these practices.

How Are We Doing
LC's goal is to cultivate strong working relationships with our customers to ensure that all requests for legal services are handled in a confidential manner. Ninety-nine percent of respondents
responded "Good" or "Excellent" when rating Legislative Counsel's success in maintaining confidentiality.
Factors Affecting Results
Maintaining client confidentiality is essential in ensuring that legislators engage in frank communications with us so that we understand what a member is seeking and can provide either the bill or
amendment a member is seeking or can provide the legal advice needed for the member to make an informed decision about the legal issue they asked us to research.  In the very rare instance when
LC management learns of a breach in confidentiality, we always look at the specific circumstances and engage in training and reviewing procedures to enhance confidentiality safeguards.  We intend
to continue actively addressing any confidentiality concerns as they arise.

How Are We Doing
LC strives to provide accurate information in a timely manner. This means producing Oregon Laws, Oregon Revised Statutes and other legislative publications in an accurate, economical and timely
manner. Ninety-eight percent of respondents responded "Good" or "Excellent" when rating the quality of the legislative publications produced by Legislative Counsel.
Factors Affecting Results
The
Oregon Revised Statutes
consists of more than 36,000 pages of the codification of Oregon Laws. LC also publishes an annual supplement reflecting short session enactments, six specialty
publications, an annotations volume, a two-volume index and the hardbound
Oregon Laws,
the constitutionally required official compendium of legislative enactments. In addition, all laws and the
codifications of those laws are available in both print and digital formats. It is extremely rare for LC to need to make corrections to these publications because of our robust compilation and publication
preparation processes. We will continue to use these processes and procedures in the preparation of our print and electronic publications.

How Are We Doing
LC strives to provide legal advice that is clear, concise and easily understood by the member requesting the advice regardless of the complexity of the subject matter. The customer satisfaction survey
showed ninety-six percent of respondents rated their satisfaction with the agency's ability to provide quality legal advice as "Good" or "Excellent".
Factors Affecting Results
Experience has shown LC management that it takes at least six years for an LC attorney to gain mastery of the practice of legislative law and familiarity with assigned subject matter areas. Similar
learning curves exist among legal editor and administrative services staff. For these reasons, we strive to retain staff and have Legislative Counsel serve as a long-term employer, so that we can
provide the quality advice our clients need within the time deadlines by which they need the advice. As our actuals exceed targets in this KPM, we intend to continue these practices.
