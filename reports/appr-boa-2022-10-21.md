Report Year 2018 2019 2020 2021 2022
TIMELY REVIEW OF NEW COMPLAINTS
Actual 0 133
Target 120 120
How Are We Doing
Average days from opening to approval was 133 days for FY 2022 (10.8% over target).  38% of reports were approved within the target range of
120 days.
Factors Affecting Results
The second investigator position vacated at the beginning of the reporting period, which caused inquiry reports to be completed later, thus
delaying approval.  In the second half of FY 2022, compliance resources were diverted to licensing in response to all icensing staff departing the
agency, in order to be able to continue basic agency operations.  This affected performance on this measure as well.
The agency expects to be able to significanly improve performance again on this measure in FY 2023.

Report Year 2018 2019 2020 2021 2022
Metric Value
Actual 100 100 100 100 73

How Are We Doing
In FY 2022, the Board did not meet all best practices measures.  The Board took action by removing its Executive Director in January 2022, and
hiring a new Director in May 2022.  The Board expects to meet all best practices measueres again starting with FY 2023.
Factors Affecting Results
The main factors decreasing the Board's performance on this measure were lack of adequately involvement in key communications and policy
decisions, and failure to adequately coordinate with and consider key stakeholder views. The Board identified those shortcomings and self-
corrected that course by terminating its Director and bringing in a new Director with a clear expecation to correct those deficiencies.
