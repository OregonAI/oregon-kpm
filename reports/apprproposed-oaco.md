How Are We Doing
OACO has onboarded six new commissioners within the 2024 calendar year. We have received five responses. We are averaging 89% for the quality of our onboarding and training process for new
commissioners.
Factors Affecting Results
We had one commissioner respond to our survey stating that they were left feeling unclear about their individual roles as  commissioners and how each commissioner can effect
change. The respondent also mentioned they did not know the Governors parameters for our advocacy work as commissioners.

How Are We Doing
OACO created a survey for customer service for the 2024 Calendar year. Overall, OACO staff are providing quality, reliable, accurate information in a timely manner to community. There are
opportunities for process improvement. At the time of updating this KPM, we have received 59 responses. Out of the 59 responses, 31 organizations and community members have interacted with us
the last year, most through attending community events. Below are the questions that we used to gather feedback for the results listed above:
.

.

.
Overall, how satisfied are you with the service provided by the Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office? 4.55/5
Factors Affecting Results
Factors that are affecting our results can be attributed to complex questions that are asked from community, and we do not always have the bandwidth or staffing to address every issue within the
community. OACO staff are dedicated to making a positive impact on the communities we represent and support. We are a small agency which limit our ability to provide the best customer service as
we have four separate advocacy commissions with only four permanent staff dedicated to supporting their work.
