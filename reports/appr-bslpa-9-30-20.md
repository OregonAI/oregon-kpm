obtain records.  For the December, 2019 biennial renewal process, the Board moved to a new database and has begun the transition to a paperless licensing process.  The Board updated the SLPA
rules effective 1/1/20. For these reasons, the Board is delaying the audit until October, 2020. The audit will also be used to clean up the new database and provide compliance guidance.

Factors Affecting Results
Since this KPM begins with the year 2020, the Board will be reporting the results of this measurement for the first time 180 days after the close of 2020. Progress so far has been good and the Board
is on target for meeting this goal. As of September 30, 2020, of the cases opened from January through June, 2020, only two are still open and the percentage closed within 180 days is 89.4%. The
Board will report final results for 2020 on June 30, 2021.
