How Are We Doing
For reporting year 2023, the board has resolved 15% of cases within six months of when the complaint is received in the board office. This is a significant increase from the prior year, but is still not up
to the target. What is not demonstrated from this statistic is that the board opened 41 cases and closed 35 cases in 2023.
Factors Affecting Results
The percent of cases resolved within six months is still below the Board’s target of 50%.  The Board faced several challenges in meeting this goal.  Vacancies on the Board has impacted the Board’s
ability to conduct business. In addition, the Board’s executive director left the agency in early 2023. The position was essentially vacant for nearly 60 days. Finally, there were a number of extremely
old cases that the Board resolved which brought down the percentage completed within the six-month period.  As these extremely old cases continue to be resolved, the Board will be able to move
closer to its six-month goal.
