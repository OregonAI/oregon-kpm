How Are We Doing
The Board acheived 100% of onsite facility inspections during the 15-17 biennium, by time of reporting.
Factors Affecting Results
While the Board has acheived 100% on site facility inspection, we are still facing difficulty completeing document and record reviews for all facilities, especially smaller cemeteries, where
coordination with vollunteer staff takes much longer than with other facilities.  These facilities present lower risk, however, as the number of interments is very low, relative to large for profit
cemeteries and funeral homes.  Additional staffing has been allocated to address the needs of this program.
