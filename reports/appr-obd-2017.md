How Are We Doing
For FY 2017 we made good progress on achieving this goal of reducing the average time to complete an investigation from the prior year. Our new dental investigator position was filled in January
2016, and with training the caseload backlog has been reduced. As identified in our 2017-2020 Strategic Plan, we will be managing retirements as 4 out of 8 staffers, are eligible to retire over the
next 3 years. One investigator chose to retire in August 2017, and we will manage that by cross-training staff, evaluating investigative processes, hiring the best candidates that apply for our
openings as they occur and working with our Licensees to manage this as effectively as possible.
Factors Affecting Results
The Board continues to see cases where the complainant has sought treatment from multiple providers, which can stretch out the time to complete investigations as we follow prescribed processes
to collect patient records, information and interview the licensees involved in providing treatment.  Certain cases that have involved multiple providers along with delays from the attorneys involved,
can also result in cases stretching on for years, instead of being resolved in a more timely manner. The Board has directed staff to hold licensees more accountable to the statutes and rules in the
Dental Practice Act regarding cooperation and timely responses to Board requests for documentaion and information on cases.

How Are We Doing
For FY 2017 the surveys we received and complied missed our ratings goals.  In compliance with the Oregon Legislative directive, the Board utilizes a Customer Service Survey as one tool to
determine the customer satisfaction with the accuracy of carrying out the statutory requirements of the Mission of the Board. Although targets were not met, the overall response is fairly positive,
and we will continue to encourage people to submit surveys and we always encourage feedback from our stakeholders.
Factors Affecting Results
People choose to respond to surveys on their own volition. We will continue to promote the survey and encourage feedback whether positive or negative. We have focused on being a more open
and resourceful agency over the past two years, and we feel that licensees, the public and our stakeholders can contact us directly to voice their concerns or share feedback, and that this open
communication line may mean less surveys are being submitted than in the past.
