How Are We Doing
In 2014, Oregon’s adult current tobacco use was slightly lower than all other states in the United States (19% versus 21%).  Since the beginning of Oregon’s Tobacco Prevention and Education
Program (TPEP) in 1997, adult cigarette smoking has declined by 25 percent. However, much work needs to be done. Tobacco use remains the number one preventable cause of death and
disease in Oregon, killing over 7,000 people each year, and costing Oregonians $2.5 billion a year in medical expenditures and lost productivity due to premature death. Results from the Behavioral
