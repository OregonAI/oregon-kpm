How Are We Doing
Factors Affecting Results
The audit usually takes place in the spring. However, the COVID-19 pandemic hit Oregon in late February, and school districts closed their doors in late March, making it difficult or impossible to
obtain records.  For the December, 2019 biennial renewal process, the Board moved to a new database and has begun the transition to a paperless licensing process.  The Board updated the SLPA
rules effective 1/1/20. For these reasons, the Board had to delay the audit until October, 2020. The majority of the audits were incomplete, so we are finishing them this fall, 2021, and will get back on
track in 2022.

How Are We Doing
Factors Affecting Results
The board opened 62 cases in 2020, all of which have gone to the Board as of August 6, 2021. Of those 62 cases, all but 5 went to the Board within 180 days. The average time for cases to go to the
Board from the date they were opened was 98 days in 2020. We were able to meet this goal by hiring a full time investigator on May 8, 2019.
