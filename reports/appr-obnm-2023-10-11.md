How Are We Doing
The Board opens an investigation into every complaint received. The Board received 34 complaints and opened 34 complaint investigations from January 1-Dec 31, 2022.  The Board completed 24 of
those investigations within the 2022 calendar year. The average time from complaint to completion of the investigation was 6.1 months.  Nine investigations from calendar year 2022 remain open as of
Sept 30, 2023. The remaining nine complaint investigations have been open for an average of 9.27 months. Five of the nine complaints came in after October 1, 2022.
Factors Affecting Results
In late 2021 and early 2022, the Board received multiple complaints with serious allegations of misconduct. Due to both the patient harm that formed the basis of the complaints, and the urgency to
protect the public from future harm, I directed the Board Investigator to expedite completion of those investigations over prior and subsequent complaint investigations with less potential for patient or
public harm. The most complex of those investigation, a misdiagnosis with devastating consequences, required 18 months of intense investigation and two experts to complete.
An example of the type of case held in abeyance, and notably the oldest of the nine-remaining open cases was submitted on Jan. 4, 2022, by an insurance investigator conducting an audit of a
licensees' billing and charting practices on Jan. 4, 2022.  Despite multiple requests, the complainant did not provide us with the name and patient information to subpoena the pertinent records until
June 2022.
The Board disciplined ten licensees in 2022, twice the amount than 2021.  Importantly, these cases all ended in consent orders, not costly administrative trials.  I believe this evidences the resources
and time spent to investigate and expedite these cases, while slightly yielding on the overall KPM timeliness target, was in the best interest of the Board, the complainant, and the public.
actual target
Lastly, the Board saw an uptick in the number of complaints submitted in 2023. As of Sept 30, 2023, the Board’s received 33 complaints, one less than the entirety of calendar year 2022.   The
increase in complaints strains the workload of the sole Board investigator.
