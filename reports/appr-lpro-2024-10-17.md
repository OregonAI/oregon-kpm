How Are We Doing
actual target
The Legislative Policy and Research Office (LPRO) continues to adhere to the office's vision, mission, and values of providing the Oregon Legislative Assembly with professional nonpartisan staffing,
research, and analysis. From 2023-2024, LPRO's
overall performance
was 96%, above the target rate of 90%. The timeliness and accuracy of services provided to members, from staffing policy
committees, completing complex research activities, supporting legislatively created task forces and workgroups, to providing language access services, continue to rank high among respondents,
with 94% indicating overall
timeliness
as being "excellent" or "good,” and 97% as “excellent” or “good” for
accuracy
. The
expertise
and
helpfulness
of LPRO staff also continue to rank above 95%.
Respondents reported that LPRO staff continue to be viewed as "experts" for our staff's multiple legislative roles and responsibilities, both in short and long sessions and in the interim. The
availability
of information
reported by respondents was rated as "excellent" or "good" by 97% of those who completed the survey. From 2020-2024, LPRO's scores for four of the five measures exceeded the
90% target for service with one exception: availability of information. In the most recent years, 2022-24, LPRO's performance on availability of information has exceeded the target of 90%.
Factors Affecting Results
In the 2023-2024 interim, LPRO staffed seven task forces, the Joint Committee on Transportation Statewide Tour, multiple workgroups, assisted policy committees, and completed over 100 member
research requests. The Language Access Team has provided ASL interpretation services to all committees during the 2023 and 24 legislative sessions and offered these services to the LPRO-staffed
task forces. LPRO maintains a searchable website with information and resources for committee services, research, and language access. LPRO requested that Information Services pull data on our
website
traffic in 2023, which received approximately 21,000 views. The most frequently viewed pages are (1) our landing page, (2) research and analysis, and (3) language access and who we are.
LRPO continues to see a high workload, in session and the interim, particularly with committee services and legislative task forces. In the interim, with varying intensity levels, LPRO assisted
legislative workgroups in the Senate and House. Legislative workgroups can require research, facilitation, and administrative support, among other typical services LPRO provides to members and
committees. With the Joint Transportation Committee Statewide Tour, staff across the office, including committee assistants, were tasked with working 10-12 hour days, weekends, and evenings to
ensure the twelve roadshows were adequately staffed.

How Are We Doing
The legislature adopted LPRO's proposed new measures in the 2023 session. This is the first reporting cycle with the new measures designed to capture a significant amount of the office’s workload,
including work in the interim. LPRO’s entire team, from legislative and research analysts to committee assistants and language access staff, have been actively assisting seven legislative task forces,
several of which meet every other week. On average, this interim, 1-2 task forces met weekly and averaged 2-4 hours per meeting. Staff involvement in task forces and workgroups has been extensive
during this interim, including policy research, analysis, and development. Related, the Language Access Team has ensured ASL services were provided at task force meetings. This involved planning
actual target
and participating in full-day meetings in 12 different geographic localities over a fourth-month period: June through the end of September. For publications, LPRO continues to release
Summaries of
Legislation
, Issues Briefs, and legislative task force reports. According to respondents, the quality of policy research (97%) exceeded the 90% benchmark LPRO proposed in 2023. The depth and
breadth of policy research services to members, task forces, and workgroups continues to expand. For Language Access, one respondent commented, “
This office always provides exceptional work,
and their language access program exceeds expectations; they are extremely professional and have never refused to fulfill a language request. Their delivery is prompt, effective, and top quality
.”
Factors Affecting Results
Among the individuals who responded to the survey, only 25-to-26 responded to the four measures. In calculating the measures, LPRO removed individuals (<10) who answered “don’t know” about
the following topics: task force staffing, language access, and legislative publications. No respondents indicate “poor” for the new measures when rating LPRO services. The new measures were not
initially included when the annual survey was administered. Legislative Administration revised the survey and asked individuals to retake it if they had already completed it. This may have contributed
to a less-than-ideal response rate when conducting the survey. Furthermore, LPRO is unaware of whether or how familiar those who responded were with the range of services LPRO provides, which
may have resulted in the low response rate. Based on the results shared with LPRO, only 33 individuals completed the questions about LPRO, with 46 respondents skipping these questions.
LPRO continues to hire exceptional legislative and research analysts and language access professionals, including a recent hire of a full-time editor. The additional staff allows the office to continue
providing robust, quality staffing services. However, the workload in the interim requires LPRO to critically assess, on an ongoing basis, whether existing levels of resources can sustain the workload.

How Are We Doing
LPRO continues to receive high marks in providing nonpartisan services to members and the branch, with 79% of respondents reporting "excellent" and 12% reporting "good."  We continue to exceed
the 90% target.
Factors Affecting Results
LPRO provides all new employees training on nonpartisanship expectations, including a review of the office's policy on nonpartisan expectations for both limited-duration session staff and continuing
staff. LPRO's nonpartisan policy provides a common understanding of nonpartisanship and objectivity expectations for our staff. Newly hired staff are assigned a mentor to guide acceptable conduct,
including avoiding partisanship or bias. In alignment with LPRO's mission, we strive to maintain an agency that is neutral, unbiased, and objective, which is imperative to foster the confidence and trust
of all members of the Oregon Legislative Assembly and the state of Oregon. In the recent interim, LPRO provided a series of officewide trainings, including working with NCSL on writing and civility
and holding workshops with nonpartisan offices.
