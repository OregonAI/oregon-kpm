How Are We Doing
The Board received 99 responses to a customer satisfaction survey sent to licensees. Eighty four of the 99 respondents said they were either very satisfied or satisfied with the customer service
provided by the Board. The other 15 respondents said they were neither satisfied nor dissatisfied. Of the 99 total responses, 92 answered the question about satisfaction with the service provided by
Board staff. Of the 92 responding, 78 said they were either very satisfied or satisfied with the service provided by Board staff. The remainder indicated they were neither satisfied nor dissatisfied. The
Board will strive to collect more survey responses in the next reporting period.
The timeliness measure is an aggregate of how quickly the Board processes licensing applications, exam applications, and responds to complaints. Of the 5,277 transactions, 5,255 were completed
or responded to within three business days.
The Board was not able to obtain feedback on the availability of information, the accuracy of information, or the expertise of staff. The Board will collect this information in the next reporting period.
