How Are We Doing
Following the 2005 legislative session, we conducted a customer satisfaction survey of the 90 members of the Legislative Assembly. In that survey, members were asked to rate our performance in
the areas of timeliness, accuracy, helpfulness, expertise and availability of information, as well as overall performance. Potential responses ranged from poor to fair to good to excellent and included
a "don't know" option. All 90 legislators were surveyed.
All scores were able to meet or exceed targeted goals.
Factors Affecting Results
With only 90 possible responses the scores can easily be skewed from one year to another depending on whether or not a member responds. Reponses have been generally declining.

How Are We Doing
The goal of 80% was not reached for the 2017 reporting period. Changes to the websites that produced a few issues and video system issues generated an understandable lack of approval. The
video system software issues have been resolved. The Information Services staff are dedicated to making positive changes to the websites and expect to see the approval rating improve.
Factors Affecting Results
Several changes were made to the website based on public feedback. These changes have the potential to create issues as well as increase approval ratings. The video system software that
supports committee and chamber rooms experienced issues.

How Are We Doing
The goal is to have no more than 20% voluntary turnover. This goal is established based on the average annual turnover for Government organizations during the last calendar year. Retention is
exceeding the goal.
Factors Affecting Results
Our retention has increased from the prior reporting period. Within state government there were fewer opportunities due to an executive order freezing hiring. Outside of state government the
economy is stable, but not growing as fast as it has over the past few years. Retirements will continue to provide turnover and compensation caps are a primary drivers to increasing turnover.

How Are We Doing
The reporting period is awards issued in 2017 for activity in the prior fiscal year. A Gold Star Certificate is award by the Oregon Department of Administrative Services Chief Financial Office for
providing accurate and complete fiscal year end information in a timely manner. Legislative Administration Financial Services perpares financial reporting for five legislative agencies. For 2017 the
certificate was earned for all five agencies.
Factors Affecting Results
In any given year, actual results may be impacted by staff turnover, resource constraints within Legislative Administration, and the extent and complexity of new accounting and financial reporting
standards set by the Governmental Accounting Standards Board (GASB).
