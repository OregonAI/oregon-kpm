How Are We Doing
Following the 2015 legislative session, the legislative branch conducted a customer satisfaction survey of the members of the Legislative Assembly and legislative staff. Survey participants were
asked to rate LC's performance in areas of timeliness, accuracy, helpfulness, expertise, information available and overall performance.
Factors that impact customer satisfaction are volume, legislative process deadlines, timeliness requirements and staff experience. All factors outside of Legislative Counsel's direct control.
Response totals show approximately 59 to 71 individuals elected to evaluate LC. Sample size and composition should be considered when evaluating this information.
