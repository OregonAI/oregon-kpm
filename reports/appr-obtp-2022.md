How Are We Doing
The Board had 236 individuals attempt the tax preparer licensing exam in 2021. Of those, 169 passed the exam and 67 did not pass. The calculation is ((236-67/236) = 71.6%
The Board believes the COVID pandemic impacted the exam pass rate in 2021 as fewer people took the exam because doing so required sitting in a relatively small room with several other
individuals for four hours. In addition, those who failed the exam the first time were reluctant to go back and retake the exam due to COVID.
