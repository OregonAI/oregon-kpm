How Are We Doing
DOJ met the target percentage.  This is a composite score of three divisions.  Civil Enforcement was at 97%, Trial Division was 23% and General Counsel Division was 26%.  The weighted averge
composite score is 32%.
Factors Affecting Results
The General Counsel Division handles hundreds of contested matters.  In those matters, settlements often cannot be reached when there is a fundamental disagreement about the facts, the law, or
strength of legal positions.  The seriousness of the conduct, type of sanction, violation history and other agency policy and business considerations also play into settlement decisions.  Many of the
General Counsel Division's contested matters not resolved by settlement result in a favorable determination for the state.
The Trial Division handles cases which include hundreds of cases each biennium in which adults in custody represent themselves and seek either money damages by alleging a variety of civil rights
and tort claims or they seek to be released due to conditions of their confinement.  Those cases are often resolved on early motions for dismissal or motions for summary judgment which skew
settlement percentages to a much lower number than the target for the Department as a whole.

How Are We Doing
The KPM for the Charitable Activities Section (CAS) was changed in 2021.  For many years, the KPM had been the percentage of registered charities that filed timely and complete reports.  That
percentage was generally around 63%, which fell short of the goal of 70%.   Although we take steps like mailing the report form before the due date and adding a checklist to those mailings so that
charities can verify their reports are complete before they send them in,  we are limited in our ability to ensure that charities accomplish the goal of filing timely and complete reports.  Accordingly, the
KPM was changed to a measurement that is more within the Department’s control and that is to promptly notifiy charities that their reports are delinquent.  For the 2021-22 fiscal year, the percentage
of registrants that are notified within 60 days that their report is delinquent was 99%.
Factors Affecting Results
The process of notifying charitable registrants that their reports are delinquent is largely automated.  Report deadlines are tied to an organization’s fiscal year.  After the deadline has elapsed, we run
reports that generate letters to the organizations that are delinquent in filing their report, notifying them of the delinquency and how to address it.  Occasionally, we may stop all correspondence to a
particular organization for administrative or legal reasons and that may account for approximately 1% of delinquent registrants not receiving notification of the delinquency.  We recently developed a
database query to enable us to identify any organization in which the stop correspondence box is checked so that we can ensure they receive appropriate notification of their delinquent status as well.

How Are We Doing
For the federal fiscal year ending September 30, 2021, the Oregon Child Support Program’s performance is 66%, exceeding the target of 65%.
This measure is necessary for federal reporting requirements and must be reported based on the federal fiscal year.
Factors Affecting Results
The amount collected depends in part on the effectiveness and efficiency of the tools available to DOJ and the Oregon Child Support Program under state and federal law for parents ordered to pay
support who are able but unwilling to meet their obligations. This measure also depends on court-ordered amounts being set at a level that allows parents to pay the full amount each month. To
receive full credit for a collection on current support due, the entire monthly court-ordered amount must be received. The number of parents paying support may increase, but this measure only
increases if the amount of money collected increases.
Economic factors impact this measure as lower incomes make collections more difficult, and higher incomes make collecting easier. The Program's effectiveness in collecting child support from
parents who have the ability to pay also depends on the resources invested to carry out collection activities. Timing of payments is a factor as well. Payments received even one day into the following
month do not count as a current support payment in the month the payment was due. Therefore, individuals on a 26-paycheck cycle (every other week) have months where the final payment on
current support comes after the end of the month. The individual may be current on support payments at the end of the year, but the collections on current support will not reach 100%.

How Are We Doing
For the federal fiscal year ending September 30, 2021, the Oregon Child Support Program's performance is 71%, anomalously exceeding the target by 6 percentage points.
This measure is necessary for federal reporting requirements and must be reported based on the federal fiscal year.
Factors Affecting Results
Distribution rules dictate the order in which money must be distributed on a case. If current support is due, the full amount of current support must be collected before money can be applied to arrears.
Monthly court-ordered amounts that are beyond the reach of a paying parent's income negatively affect this measure by creating arrears that the parent is then unable to pay toward. Economic factors
also influence this measure. During time of economic strength, collections typically increase and the chance of receiving an amount greater than current support increases. Conversely, a poor
economy typically leads to a decrease in collections and an increase in arrears owed. This measure is tied to measure 12, and an increase in measure 12 is the most likely way to increase measure
13.
FFY 2020 and 2021 were anomalies for this measure because the first individual stimulus payment was offset for past-due child support. The offsets began in FFY 2020 and continued into FFY 2021,
with stimulus payments for joint filers being held for six months and released in FFY 2021. This means that many more cases than usual received a payment toward arrears, raising the percentage
much higher than the target. It’s anticipated that this measure will be closer to the target percentage going forward.
