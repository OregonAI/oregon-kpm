Factors Affecting Results
During the 2017 legislative session, this KPM was amended.  Before 2017, the KPM measured how many applications are processed within ten days.  The new KPM measures how many
applications are processed within five days.
Some of the renewals that were not completed within five days due to an ongoing malfunction with the online renewal system, which keeps online renewals stuck in the queue for several days, with
licensing staff unable to identify and pull the renewal.  This agency is working with several other licensing boards to transition to a new database which would correct this problem.

How Are We Doing
At this point, almost 90 percent of the applications that can renew online are doing so.  But there are some categories of license and permit that cannot renew online, which drags down the total
online renewals to 53%.  Over time, with updates to our database, the types of individuals who must renew through the mail will decrease, and online renewals (as a % of total renewals) will
increase.
Factors Affecting Results
Limited permits account for about ten percent of total licenses, and must be processed through the mail for renewal.  That is because OBMI audits continuing education for all limited permit
renewals, and documentation of CE completion must be attached to each permit renewal application.  In the future, when this agency gets an updated database, it should be possible to have a
function that allows CE documentation to be attached electronically to online renewals, which will reduce or eliminate the need for most mail-in renewals.
