How Are We Doing
This measure is holding steady with where we have been the last couple years. Starting in late 2023, OVMEB Inspectors dug through a longstanding backlog of cases stemming from increase in
complaints from 2020-2021. From 2016 to 2020, the highest number of cases received was 67. Cases nearly doubled the next year and have increased and remained high since. In 2023, we brought
on a second inspector to help handle the workload. At that time, we had dozens of cases that were 3 and 4 years old. Today, we have only 6 cases over a year old, and these are due to hang ups due
to hearings requests other administrative delay.

How Are We Doing
actual target
Most of our percentages are very similar to what was previously reported. We also noticed an uptick in direct positive commentary about specific staff members. This is reflected in an increase in
positive Timeliness responses. We feel this is a result of our increased efforts in trying to return calls within a timely manner, as directed by the Governor. We have increased our direct response
times, and respondents seem to appreciate the direct interaction.
Factors Affecting Results
OVMEB conducts an online survey to obtain this feedback. The survey is available on our agency website, and in all staff email signatures. As of October of 2025, we received 29 responses. This is a
slight uptick to the 24 responses obtained in 2024.
We did note an increase in survey participation in the spring and summer of 2025  from members of an active social media group who were sharing their negative views of one particular practicing
licensee, and their perception that the agency was “doing nothing”. These responses pulled down our Overall measure slightly.
We also feel that a survey of this type will not accurately capture much of the positive impact we have on our licensees, stakeholders and the public. Emotional impact, and subsequent negativity bias,
is often involved when dealing with people, their pets and complaints to our agency. Also, self-selection and non-response bias: Many surveys and online reviews rely on voluntary participation. The
people who choose to respond, or "self-select," often have stronger feelings about the topic than the general population. This leads to a higher proportion of extremely negative feedback, while more
moderate opinions are underrepresented.
However, while not perfect, we value the direct response we get from the public through this survey. It helps us measure where we are and where we can continue to improve. We continue to look for
ways to increase participation and get this survey out to a broader swath of the people we engage with and serve.

How Are We Doing
This is our newest KPM. This is an extremely important measure, as it reflects our turnaround time for processing a license application. Our licensing process strives to ensure we provide proper
oversight, while also ensuring efficient process time so applicants can get tow work. We receive approximately 300-350 new applications per year. We have one licensing administrator to process
those. This also requires a background check, which requires additional time and tasking. Depending on when within the week we receive a completed application packet, we often process an
application within the same day. Our staff is incredibly responsive to applicants. We want to process these as quickly as possible, without sacrificing due diligence and public safety.
Factors Affecting Results
We will continue tracking monthly, quarterly and yearly application numbers. We will continue to review our process for more efficient ways to process license applications appropriately.
