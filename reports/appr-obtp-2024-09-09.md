KPM #1 Processing license and registration applications - Percent of license and registration applications processed within 3 days of receipt.
Data Collection Period: Jan 01 - Jan 01
* Upward Trend = positive result
Report Year 2020 2021 2022 2023 2024
Processing license and registration applications
Actual 99.50% 99.67% 99.13% 98% 99.66%
Target 99% 99% 99% 99% 99%
How Are We Doing
The Board processed 4,396 applications in 2023.  This is 300+ more applications than the Board received last year.  Yet the Board only did not process 15 of those within three days of receipt.   The
calculation is (4396-15/4396) = 99.66
More than 90% of applications are currently processed online and completed the same day.

Processing exam applications
Actual 99.18% 99.10% 99.32% 98.40% 98.37%

How Are We Doing
The Board processed 612 exam applications in 2023. This is 158 more applications than the Board received last year.  Yet the Board only did not process 10 of those within three days of receipt.   The
calculation is (612-10/612) = 98.37
The Board continues to strive to meet its 3-day goal to process all applications but with the influx in both license and exam applications and being a staff of only two we have found it to be a
challenge to continually maintain this goal.
