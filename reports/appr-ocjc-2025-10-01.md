How Are We Doing
actual target
The Criminal Justice Commission (CJC) has conducted annual customer service surveys since 2007. These surveys focus on the major areas of the CJC's work and means of contact with our
customers, primarily grantees. The CJC's ratings have been consistently good or excellent for the last decade. For the 2025 survey, the agency saw a decrease in all categories, however the scores
are generally in the range reported over the last 5 years. During this time the agency has recently invested in refining and improving its administration of the programs and research divisions. With the
increased number of projects, grant programs, and staff, the agency has developed new processes to administer the increasing complexity. This survey data was collected beginning August 2025.
Factors Affecting Results
The agency's service delivery roles are two-fold: first, the CJC provides rigorous data analyses to inform criminal justice policy work; and second, the CJC administers grants to support the
improvement of local criminal justice systems. Both parts of the agency require staff to administer these aims with transparency, consistency, and impartially, so that customers trust the reliability of
data analyses performed and have confidence that decisions regarding grant funding are based on fair criteria and accurate information. While CJCs 2025 customer service survey results were below
targets, the agency has experienced an increased number of grant programs, research projects, and complexity of existing projects may have been part of our target goal for customer service. While
CJC is not the only agency to struggle with new projects and recruitment challenges, the agency has a roadmap for creating a more uniform process for our clients to try and reduce the confusion and
complications with the multitude of grants being delivered by the agency.

How Are We Doing
This metric examines the percentage of the CJC’s grant programs that met or exceeded 75% of grant requirements, meaning the percentage of grant programs completing all required grant
deliverables. The current target is 95% compliance with the 75% threshold. Data were collected in 2025 for 2023-25 grant awards. For the CJC’s grant programs, the agency evaluates the compliance
of program administration, progress reporting, annual reporting, and fiscal reimbursement and/or reconciliation reports including timeliness and percentage complete. Grant program staff offer
technical assistance to ensure reports are completed on time and accurately.
Factors Affecting Results
An increase in the number of grant recipients meeting or exceeding grant requirements can be attributed to staff efforts to provide exceptional attention to detail on grant deliverables and timely
assistance to grant recipients, as well as actively improving processes for efficiency. CJC staff utilize a combination of regular progress reporting, site visits, and expenditure tracking to support grant
recipients’ success in meeting requirements and delivering services as identified in CJC grant agreements.
