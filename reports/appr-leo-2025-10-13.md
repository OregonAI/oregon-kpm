How Are We Doing
actual target
The agency was near target in the areas of “helpfulness” and “expertise” and close to target in “accuracy,” “timeliness” and “overall.” While only 60% of customers rated their satisfaction of the
“availability of information” as excellent and good; 24% of customers rated “don’t know” on this factor. Significant effort was made in 2025 to upgrade the agency’s website making it more user-friendly
and providing more resources to the capitol community.
Factors Affecting Results
Unlike other non-partisan legislative offices, the Legislative Equity Office is unique in that it is an enforcement entity; investigating allegations of harassment, discrimination and retaliation pursuant to
Legislative Branch Personnel Rule 27 and its statutory mandate. Satisfactory outcomes for one side equals unsatisfactory outcomes for the other. Lastly, harassment, discrimination and retaliation
often provoke strong positive and negative feelings entirely unrelated to the effectiveness and efficiency of the office. While the Legislative Equity Office will always aim for a high rating in customer
satisfaction, its expectations are realistic due to the nature of the work.
