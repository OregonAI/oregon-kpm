How Are We Doing
The board is currently working with staff to compile data from July1, 2016 through June 30, 2017. We expect to have data available by November 1, 2017 and will update the report as soon as data
is available.
For reporting year 2016 the board has only resolved approximately 8% of complaints within 6 months of receipt. In 2015, the completion rate was also 8%. In prior years, the results were higher,
67% in 2012, 63% in 2013 and 23% in 2014. The board is proposing to revise this measure to more accurately reflect both our actual work and a more realistic time-frame to resolve complaints. As
measured by this performance measure, these results are not acceptable. However, this performance measure was developed at a time when the board had only one license type and far fewer
licensees.
Factors Affecting Results
The number of complaints that the board can resolve within 6 months is affected by a number of factors. First, the complexity of the cases received by the board today. Second, the board currently
has two vacancies, which can result in our not being able to meet the quorum requirement and being forced to postpone items to a future meeting. Third, the board's protocol for receiving,
investigating and presenting cases to the board for resolution often takes several months, before the earliest date it can be presented to the board. Fourth, while working through its backlog of
cases, the board continues to receive complaints. Each case is evaluated for the possiblity of resulting in client or other harm. Cases where there is a higher possiblity of client or other harm are
given priority and are processed immediately; cases that are evaluated as being less serious complaints are moved back on the calendar. Finally, prior to this reporting period, the board's
actual target
compliance manager/investigator went on maternity leave; shortly after her return, the board's other investigator left abruptly. While the position was later filled, the maternity leave, staff turn-over
and retraining a new hire has slowed our progress toward meeting the target

How Are We Doing
All Licensees are required to complete continuing education as a prerequisite for renewal. The renewal process occurs during the licensee's birth month and requires licensees to indicate the hours
of CE they have completed.
Factors Affecting Results
Each monthly renewal cycle, 20% of renewing licensees are randomly selected for continuing education audit. These renewing licensees must submit to our office evidence demostrating completion
of all continuing education. Licenses are not renewed until licensees have completed the required continuing education.

Our customer service measures show significant variation from year to year. 2016 the results are lower than in the previous year.
Factors Affecting Results
Very few licensees complete the customer service survey, which means that only one or two negative responses can skew the overall results. During this reporting period, the agency was faced
