How Are We Doing
As of August 30, 2025, the Commission had opened 260 complaints in 2025. Of these, two had been open for more than six months. There were an additional 79 open cases which were still under
the six month mark. There were also two cases remaining open from 2024.
Factors Affecting Results
Overall complaint numbers continue to increase each year, and that trend has continued this year. The Commission increased staffing from 0.5 FTE to 1 FTE. This has allowed the Commission to
continue to manage complaints in a timely manner, though a further increase in complaints will test that capacity.
