How Are We Doing
Much improved!  This is the first time that all key indicators of satisfaction have exceeded the target of 90% satisfaction by significant margins.  The Policy Analyst support of the equity
advocacy of the Advocacy Commissions has added a base of reliable expertise and availability that has increased the customer satisfaction by the Advocacy Commissioners to the
highest levels since 2010.
Factors Affecting Results
Beginning in 2015, the legislature invested in the OACO's first professional line staff with a .5 FTE Policy Analyst that brings the OACO staffing to 2.5 FTE along with the Administrator and
Executive Assistant.  The Policy Analyst support of the equity advocacy of the Advocacy Commissions has added a base of reliable expertise and availability that has increased the customer
satisfaction by the Advocacy Commissioners to the highest level in 10 years.   The Policy Analyst, in his first 9 months has improved the ability of the OAC's to track their targeted bills with
weekly legislative updates during session for the OAC's along with much more immediate assistance for each Commission in research and preparing testimony during session, as well as
new training opportunities on ways to be effective during interim in the Strategic Priority areas of the Advocacy Commissions.   His new role and added skills within the OACO office has been
the leading factor in the increase in customer satisfaction feedback.

How Are We Doing
The Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office ratings for best practices remains consistently at 100% achievement for each of 15 measures of its business processes including financial
reporting, staff management and evaluation, accounting excellence, and other accountability measures as monitored by the Oregon Commission on Asian and Pacific Islander Affairs, Black
Affairs, Hispanic Affairs and Commission for Women along with the OACO Administrator.
Factors Affecting Results
Consistent, seasoned staffing from the OACO, excellent shared professional support in accounting, HR, Risk Management and budgeting, and mindful appointments of policy oriented
Commissioners from the API community have helped achieve this level of attainment in best practices.
