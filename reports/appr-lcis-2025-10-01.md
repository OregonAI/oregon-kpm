How Are We Doing
actual target
The Legislative Commission on Indian Services (LCIS) is a three-person legislative agency which strives to provide optimal service to the public, Oregon’s nine tribal governments, multiple executive
and legislative branch offices, government officials, and representatives of business and various organizations. Staff supports the 14-member Commission to work on ways to improve state-Tribal
relations for the benefit of all Oregonians. We will continue to maximize resources to educate, advise, and assist across a vast spectrum of areas that touch on Tribes, Indians, and the State of Oregon
and fulfill a function in a way that is unique to the State of Oregon
Factors Affecting Results
LCIS has continued to make advances in providing excellent service to the public, Oregon’s nine tribal governments, multiple executive and legislative branch offices, government officials, and
representatives of business and various organizations. We continue to receive frequent positive feedback from agency and tribal partners at meetings, events we sponsor, via email, and in person. To
more accurately match the LCIS customer satisfaction survey with the somewhat unique services and products we provide to our partners, the LCIS survey varies in format somewhat from the
measures in the recording chart above. This allows us to give us a more complete and useful picture of how we are doing. The LCIS survey is meant to obtain feedback
about the priorities our state and Tribal partners want addressed and to have a guide to assess areas in which we provide the most assistance. Our goal is to provide the highest quality services and to
focus on the services most valued by our very diverse public, private, and tribal partners.
