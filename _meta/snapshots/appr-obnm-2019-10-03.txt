How Are We Doing
OBNM hired a new investigator in April 2019.  Over the last six months he cut the back log of open investigations from 23 to 12, nearly half of the outstanding cases.  He is organized, able to spot
issues and efficiently investigate cases to both my and the Board's satisfication.  I anticipate his being able to move through the outstaning cases in timely manner, and expect that the newly opened
investigations will be completed with-in the 6th month KPM next reporting period.
Factors Affecting Results
The OBNM investigator on staff for the majority of this reporting period did not meet my or the Board's performance expectations.  I worked with the OBNM investigator extensively during this period,
as well as the year prior, including providing her with ample outside training programs, in hopes of improving her output, product and timeliness.  Unfortunately, the amount of counsel and training
took up valuable time she should have been conducting investigations.  In an attempt to make up that time, I delegated several of her other tasks to co-workers to allow her to focus solely on
investigations.  However, after several months of monitoring, training, as well as the reduction of her responsibilities, she continued to miss deadlines, her output and work product was not
satisfactory, and there were consistent delays getting investigations completed and approved to presented to the Board.   Investigator submitted her resignation in Feb 2019.  Recruitment for a new
investigator was initiated immediately and a new investigator was hired in April 2019.  With a few exceptions, the majority of investigations were put on hold from February through April during the
recruitment, hiring and training of the new investigator.
