Factors Affecting Results
Best Practices:

1. Executive Director’s performance expectations are current.

2. Executive Director receives annual performance feedback.

3. The agency’s mission and high-level goals to maintain the integrity of the judiciary are current and applicable.

4. The Commission reviews the Annual Performance Progress Report.

5. The Commission is involved in review of agency’s key communications.

6. The Commission reviews all proposed and current budgets.

7. The Commission is appropriately accounting for resources.
