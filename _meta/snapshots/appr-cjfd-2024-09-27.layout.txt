How Are We Doing
As of August 30, 2024, the Commission had opened 215 cases in 2024. Of these, five cases were open for more than six months. There were an additional 60 open cases which were still under the
six month mark as of August 30, 2024.

Factors Affecting Results
The Commission had a significant case backlog at various times in 2024, but still managed to resolve most cases in under six months. Staff has been authorized and funded to work additional hours
to address the backlog.
