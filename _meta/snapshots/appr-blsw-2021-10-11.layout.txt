How Are We Doing
For reporting year 2021, the board has resolved approximately 18% of cases withing 180 days of receipt. While this represents an increase from last year, it is not yet up to the target. What is not
demostrated from this metric is that the board was able to adjudicate more cases during this time period than in past years and is on target to clear even more in the current period. The board is
continuing to refine its compliance process to meet the target.

Factors Affecting Results
The number of complaints that the board can resolve within 180 days is affected by a number of factors. First, the complexity of the cases received by the board. Second, the board currently has
two vacant positions, which increases the likelihood that meetingis will have to be cancelled for lack of a quorum. Three board positions will need to be filled in the upcoming year. It is critical to the
board's ability to resolve cases that these positions are filled. Third, the board has modified its protocol for addressing cases to allow, where appropriate, for earlier action. Finally the board has hired a
limited duration investigator to focus attention on the case backlog, while allowing existing staff to focus attention on more urgent cases involving the possibility of client or other harm.

How Are We Doing
All licensees are required to complete continuing education as a prerequisite for renewal. The renewal process occurs during the licensee's birth month and required licensees to indicate the hours of
continuing education they have completed.

Factors Affecting Results
Each monthly renewal cycle, 20% of renewing licensees are randomly selected for audit of the continuing education hours they have submitted. These renewing licensees must submit to our office
evidence demonstrating completion of all required continuing education. Licenses are not renewed until licensees have completed and submitted evidence of having completed the required continuing
education.
