How Are We Doing
11.39% of the workforce represents 9 employees who voluntarily left the Legislative Administration agency. Of those, 5 transferred to the Executive Branch for promotional opportunities; 1 retired; and
2 resigned for family or personal reasons.
We have taken steps to improve employee retention this year and anticipate a lower turnover rate next year when we return to working in the Capitol building.
Factors Affecting Results
The size of the agency is relatively small resulting in percentages being significantly impacted by a single employee.
The Target reduced this year from 20% to 10%, which is a significant percentage.
