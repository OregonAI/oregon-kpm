How Are We Doing
actual target
Following the 2017 legislative session, the legislative branch conducted a customer satisfaction survey of the members of the Legislative Assembly and legislative staff.
Survey responses indicate that LPRO is near achieving its targets. LPRO's survey questions recieved between 43 and 52 responses. This sample size should be considered when evaluating our
performance.
LPRO was formerly part of Legislative Administation. As a new agency, this is our first KPM report and these results are a baseline for future performance.
Factors Affecting Results
A number of factors impact customer satisfaction, including the volume of work and legislative process deadlines. These factors are largely outside of LPRO's direct control. LPRO hired a significant
number of new staff this past year and staff experience levels may also impact customer satisfaction.
