Excellent and good ratings remain at our near targets, though we did experience an overall drop in some categories from 2019.

The complexity and duration of the pay equity analysis & market studies, as well as effects of the pandemic seem to be the primary factors impacting results.

Additionally, results fluctuate between long session and short session years. During long session years there are more respondents and many of the respondents include comments related to the
legislative process satisfaction.
