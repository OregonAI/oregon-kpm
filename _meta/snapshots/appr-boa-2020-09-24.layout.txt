How Are We Doing
The agency assessment of customer service perofrmance for FY 20 continues to meet or exceed the targets in all categories.

Factors Affecting Results
The Board has recognized the need that information should be readily available and accessible to the public, licensee, applicants and candidates.  Earlier this year our agency undertook a whole
revamp of our website, including removing outdated information and adding relevant content.  This will provide the users to enjoy a more streamlined experience.
