How Are We Doing
actual target
The Legislative Fiscal Office (LFO) is a non-partisan legislative service agency that provides objective research, analysis, and recommendations related to the state’s biennial budget; prepares fiscal
impact statements on proposed measures; assists in development of the Legislature’s adopted balance budget; provides staff support to the Joint Committee on Ways and Means and several other
legislative committees; and publishes detailed analyses and summary documents on the budget. Access to accurate, timely, and objective fiscal information is critical to the legislative process and the
adoption of a balanced budget each session.
LFO’s customer service key performance measure (KPM) assesses the percentage of customers that rate LFO’s customer service, including timeliness, accuracy, helpfulness, expertise, and
availability of information, as good or excellent rather than fair or poor. This data is captured in an annual customer service satisfaction survey sent by Legislative Administration to Legislators and their
staff, legislative agency employees, and the lobby. There were a total of 40 respondents to LFO’s 2025 survey, an increase from the 28 responses received on the 2024 survey. Given the relatively
few number of respondents, individual responses have an impact on the overall scores. Overall customer satisfaction increased in 2025, though remains below the 90% target, along with actuals for
accuracy, timeliness, and availability of information.
Factors Affecting Results
The number and complexity of measures and amendments each session impact LFO’s workload. In addition, the number of Emergency Board meetings and special sessions impact workload and
LFO’s ability to produce budget briefs and other materials during the interim.
LFO must balance the overall number of staff with the varying volume of work between the interim and session. This means that most staff have a substantial workload in session. Experience is critical
in managing this workload, so staff turnover has a direct impact on LFO’s performance outcomes, but has been mitigated through an emphasis on new employee onboarding and training.
