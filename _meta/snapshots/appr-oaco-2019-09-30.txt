How Are We Doing
The OACs are maintaining a high level of satisfaction in all areas, having met or exceeded all key indicators of satisfaction. The comments that are submitted along with the satisfaction survey
indicate a high level of satisfaction with the clarity and breadth of information provided in the onboarding experience. Commissioners feel that they are ready for their service on the Advocacy
Commissions. Overall satisfaction has maintained significantly high levels at 95%.
Factors Affecting Results
The success of the individual commissions in effectively researching and making policy recommendations for legislators and department heads in key areas is now regularly informing several bills
each session, most recently in the areas of education, mental health, and credentialing for immigrants with advanced degrees from their home countries. The success is reflected in the consistently
high level of satisfaction by the Commissioners of OACO staff support and the overall policy outcomes of their work.

Factors Affecting Results
The Chairs and Vice Chairs of the Advocacy Commissions meet regularly and work closely with the OACO Administrator to assure that all expectations of management and leadership are met and
that the full Commissions are involved in key areas of budget review and approval, commissioner training, reporting and other required benchmarks of best practice. The review and data collection
of each area of best practice is considered by the Chair of each Commission each year at the time of the OACO Administrator evaluation.
