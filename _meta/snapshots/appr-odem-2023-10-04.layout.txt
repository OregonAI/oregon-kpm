How Are We Doing
ODEM is consistently exceeding targets across customer service KPM focus areas however engagement rates are lagging at just 75 participants.

Factors Affecting Results
1. Late adopters - ODEM lacked technology to mandate adoption of the survey link in employee email signatures and still lacks ability to identify staff who fail to include the survey link.

2. Request for contact resoltuion - ODEM is still developing processes to support those who request to be contacted regarding their experience.
