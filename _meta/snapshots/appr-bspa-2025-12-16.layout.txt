How Are We Doing
BSPA conducts its customer service survey with an online survey connected to the website and sent out with all reply emails. A link connects the person with a survey form created via Google Forms.
The survey results are from 7/1/24 to 6/1/25 and there were 35 responses. Efficiencies in the licensing database contribute to the increase in positive survey results. The agency has received many
positive comments along with the survey results.

Factors Affecting Results
We need a better system for surveying and will work on that in 2026. We want to tie the questions more closely to the specific customer service procedures and policies of Governor Kotek's
expectations.

How Are We Doing
The SLPA audit will be sent out in June every year. Using work locations we sent audit requests to the SLPs and SLPAs at 3 school districts and on Educational Service District which resulted in
responses from 59 SLPs and 41 SLPAs. This is a new KPM and due to the complexity of the responses we are unable to report results until the end of 2025. The target is to have at least 90%
compliance with Oregon Administrative Rules related to SLPA supervision.

Factors Affecting Results
New KPM, will be conducting the audit differently in 2025 than in the past. Previous SLPA audits selected entire school districts and would have a percentage of compliance with our rules. However
the rules place the responsibility of proper supervision and documentation on the SLP supervisor and SLPA, so our audits will address individuals instead of school districts.

Actual

How Are We Doing
This is a new KPM starting with calendar year 2025. In June we sent out audit letters for 3 school districts and 1 educational service district. We received responses over into the month of July.
However due to the complexity of the responses and requests for follow-up information we do not yet have results. The target is to have 100% compliance with Oregon Statutes requiring proper
licensure and parental notification. The agency expects to have results before the end of 2025.

Factors Affecting Results
New KPM, will be conducting the audit differently in 2025 than in the past. Previous SLPA audits selected entire school districts and would have a percentage of compliance with our rules. As we
separate out our audit of individual supervision, we can focus the district audit on issues such as unlicensed practice, policies, improper remote supervision, and lack of documentation in regards to
parental notification for SLPA services and telepractice.

How Are We Doing
Governor Kotek set out agency expectations three years ago, and BSPA has met all of them. Director Haag has reported Board progress on each of these expectations at the majority of quarterly
Board meetings over that period of time, and each of her reports have been reviewed and approved by the Board during public session. See here for the Governor’s list of expectations:
https://www.oregon.gov/das/strategy/pages/accountability.aspx#:~:text=Governor%20Kotek%20has%20a%20vision,and%20support%20Oregonians%20rely%20on. The Board also reviews a list of
board best practices during orientation at least every other year, generally during the May meeting. This year it was delivered on May 2, 2025 to seven Board members. The topics include : Are able to
work with a group to make decisions; Understand and follow democratic processes; Are willing to devote time and effort to board work; Have good communication skills; recognize that the board’s
goal is to serve and protect the public; Are aware that the board’s authority is granted to the board as a whole, not to individual members; Avoid becoming involved in the daily functions of the staff;
Delay making judgments until adequate evidence has been examined; Don’t let personal feelings affect decisions; Protect confidentiality; Direct all inquiries regarding board matters to the board office
so that they can be considered at a duly-constituted meeting; Refrain from discussing or releasing any details of board activities until they become part of the public record. Strictly refrain from
conducting private meetings outside of board meetings, or discussing the details of any disciplinary action with the subject of the complaint or with any other member of the public, except at a duly-
constituted board meeting and with the approval of legal counsel; Recognize that they represent the board to the public and conduct themselves accordingly, never speaking for the board unless the
board specifically authorizes them to do so.
