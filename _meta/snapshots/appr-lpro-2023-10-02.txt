How Are We Doing
actual target
The Legislative Policy and Research Office (LPRO) continues to adhere to the office's vision, mission, and values of providing the Oregon Legislative Assembly with professional nonpartisan staffing,
analysis, and research. From 2022-2023, LPRO's overall performance increased by 1.5%. The timeliness of services provided to members, from staffing policy committees, completing complex
research activities, supporting legislatively created task forces and workgroups, to providing language access services, continue to rank high among respondents with 95.1% indicating overall
timeliness as being "excellent" or "good." The expertise among LPRO staff and helpfulness also continue to rank above 95%. Respondents reported that LPRO staff continue to be viewed as "experts"
for our staff's multiple legislative roles and responsibilities, both in short and long sessions and in the interim. The availability of information reported by respondents was rated as "excellent" or "good"
by 92.7% of those who completed the survey. From 2020-2023, LPRO's actual scores have exceeded the 90% target for service with one exception: availability of information. In the most recent
years, 2022 and 2023, LPRO's performance on availability of information has exceeded the 90% target.
Factors Affecting Results
In the 2022-2023 interim, LPRO staffed six task forces, multiple workgroups, and dozens of policy committees, completed over 100 member research requests, and created a language access team to
provide spoken language and ASL interpretation and translation services. We also launched a new website with information and resources for committee services, research, and language access,
including offering a search tool.

How Are We Doing
LPRO continues to receive high marks in providing nonpartisan services to members and the branch, with 83% of respondents reporting "excellent" and 12.3% reporting "good."  We continue to
exceed the 90% target.
Factors Affecting Results
LPRO provides all new employees training on nonpartisanship expectations, including a review of the office's policy on nonpartisan expectations for session and continuing staff. LPRO's nonpartisan
policy provides a common understanding of nonpartisanship and objectivity expectations for our staff. Newly hired staff are assigned a mentor to guide acceptable conduct, including avoiding
partisanship or bias. In alignment with LPRO's mission, we strive to maintain an agency that is neutral, unbiased, and objective, which is imperative to foster the confidence and trust of all members of
the Oregon Legislative Assembly and the state of Oregon.
