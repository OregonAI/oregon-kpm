How Are We Doing
actual target
The Legislative Fiscal Office (LFO) is a non-partisan legislative service agency that provides objective research, analysis, and recommendations related to the state’s biennial budget; prepares
fiscal impact statements on proposed measures; assists in development of the Legislature’s adopted balance budget; provides staff support to the Joint Committee on Ways and Means and
several other legislative committees; and publishes detailed analyses and summary documents on the budget. Access to accurate, timely, and objective fiscal information is critical to the
legislative process and the adoption of a balanced budget each session. LFO’s customer service key performance measure (KPM) assesses the percentage of customers that rate LFO’s
customer service, including timeliness, accuracy, helpfulness, expertise, and availability of information, as good or excellent rather than fair or poor. This data is captured in an annual customer
service satisfaction survey sent by Legislative Administration to Legislators and their staff, legislative agency employees, and the lobby. There were a total of 38 respondents to LFO’s 2023
survey, an increase from the 25 responses received on the 2022 survey. Given the relatively few number of respondents, individual responses have an impact on the overall scores. Customer
satisfaction increased across all areas in 2023.
Factors Affecting Results
The number and complexity of measures and amendments each session impact LFO’s workload. In addition, the number of Emergency Board meetings and special sessions impact workload
and LFO’s ability to produce budget briefs and other materials during the interim. Considerable increases in the May revenue forecast in 2021 and 2023, as well as the influx of federal
revenues related to the pandemic also added to the complexity of finalizing the adopted budget during those legislative sessions.
LFO must balance the overall number of staff with the varying volume of work between the interim and session. This means that most staff have a substantial workload in session. Experience
is critical in managing this workload, so staff turnover has a direct impact on LFO’s performance outcomes, but has been mitigated through an emphasis on new employee onboarding
and training.
