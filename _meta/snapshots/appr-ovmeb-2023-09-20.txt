How Are We Doing
OVMEB has not met this KPM.
Factors Affecting Results
Since 2019 there has been a consistent wave of animal adoptions and purchases. With this, the need for veterinary care that increased to meet the demand.
There has also been a deline in licensed providers nationally. As a result, the OVMEB has been inundated with a consistent near a doubling of complaints year by year.
Our Policy Option Package within our 2023-25 Agency Request Budget included a new investigator to help work through and maintain the heavy volume of complaints. That person has been hired, is
continuing their training schedule. This will help reduce the time it will take to investigate and close cases.
