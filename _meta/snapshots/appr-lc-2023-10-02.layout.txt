How Are We Doing
LC's goal is to provide premium legal and publication services to the Legislative Assembly. In September 2023, a customer satisfaction survey was conducted to rate the overall service of the agency.
The survey was sent to Legislators and legislative staff. There were 66 respondents who rated the Legislative Counsel section of the survey. The percentage of respondents who rated Customer
Service as "Good" or "Excellent" for Timeliness - 97%; Accuracy - 97%; Helpfulness - 98%; Expertise - 97%; Overall Quality of Services - 98%. One hundred percent of respondents answered that the
Quality of Services Provided by Legislative Counsel were "Getting Better" or "Staying the Same".
