How Are We Doing
Our inspection pace slowed the second half of the 15-17 biennium, based on overall workload and the need to focus resources on higher-risk inspections and investigations.  FY 2018 is expected
to return to full inspection rate, especially when viewing the metric from a rolling biennium total.
Factors Affecting Results
This single metric includes inspection of all facilities and facility records, including funeral homes, cemeteries, crematories and alternative disposition facilities.  Cemeteries, in particular, our largest
and most varied license type, have proven somewhat challenging in receiving requisite information back from the facilities in order to complete the records inspections for those facilities.  We have
been working to streamline the process and assist the often volunteer cemetery staff in complying with the records requests.

Also, as noted above, due to needing to staff other priorities related to investigations and related inspections, we were short-staffed for standard inspections.  These drivers have passed and
inspections will return to full capacity.  We are also implementing strategies to close the inspection gap, to make up lost ground in our per year inspection rate.

Factors Affecting Results
The Board has previously identified the need to change the collection mechanism for the customer service survey, which has increasingly seen lower and lower response rates, thereby undermining
the accuracy of the metric as an accurate reflection of stakeholder satisfaction with the Board and the Board's level of customer service. As an example, the only 18 responses total were received
between July 2016 and June 2017.

To address this, the Board is seeking to implement a pushed survey to all stakeholders at the end of the review period, rather than continue to rely upon passive survey collection, using a link to a
survey in emails from board staff.  As part of this shift, the board is also shifting the collection period from a fiscal year to a calendar year, based on feedback from stakeholders who recommended
such a change to make it easier to track the feedback period.

Therefore, the data for this metric is expected in Jan/Feb 2018.  Surveys will be distributed in late December/early January.
