How Are We Doing
The Board of Accountancy has exceeded all targets regarding customer service during the period of July 1, 2020 through June 30, 2021.

Factors Affecting Results
The Board has implemented during our last renewal cycle (beginning May 2021 through August 2021) a portal in which our licensees can upload their documents and pay online.  This new process
was extremly helpful to our licensees and the feedback was extrodinary.  I believe this is the part of the reason our numbers for customer service were so high.
