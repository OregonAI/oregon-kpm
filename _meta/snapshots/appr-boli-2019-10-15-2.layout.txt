Factors Affecting Results
ATD has restructured staff duties to increase its efficiency and capacity.  In the last year, ATD now has a dedicated compliance unit tasked with conducting annual affirmative action compliance
reviews on its programs.  In addition, ATD’s technical assistance unit is focused on providing guidance and assistance to all registered apprenticeship programs, including resources and assistance
to programs on their EEO efforts.  Every program with more than 4 apprentices is required to have goals and timetables for women and minority participation, and submit an affirmative action work
plan detailing the programs efforts in recruitment, retention and completion for women and minorities.
