Target                                                                         85%                               85%                               85%                               85%                               85%

How Are We Doing
The current federal benchmark for collections is 75%.   Looking at the historic data on this KPM going back to 2000 we have consistently been between 75 and 77%.  The 85% target
should be considered an aspirational goal, but it may not be the correct metric in which we are measured.  For FY2027/28 I will be proposing that we reassess this goal going forward.

Factors Affecting Results
Last year there were a total of 18 offices that had a child support division.   For varrious reasons but primairly budget motivated, three of the offices have turned collections over to the
State.   These three officies collectively were above average in their collections, so thier loss has pulled the average down.   An additional factor affecting the results is a shift in focus
from pure debt collection to a social services approach with the goal of "supporting parents to support children."

Most offices report 100% compliance with this goal.  Last year was 97% so we are in a good place here.

Factors Affecting Results
Those offices that report less than 100% indicate challenges with uncooperative victims who refuse to show up for grand jury, as well as difficulty in locating victims who do not have
stable addresses for notification.

Actual                                                                        88%                              92%                               83%                              90%                               80%
Target                                                                        95%                              95%                               95%                              95%                               95%

How Are We Doing
This is a drop from last year to near 2023 levels.

Factors Affecting Results
All of the counties that do not have early resolution programs have a lower volume of cases and are among the smallest of counties by population.  Since early resolution programs
requires the cooperation of the Court, these smaller counties simply have decided the programs are not worth the time and expense.  This is also a KPM that should be reevaluated
going forward.
