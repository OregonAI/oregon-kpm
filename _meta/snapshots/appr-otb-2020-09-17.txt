How Are We Doing
The Board processed 4.547 license and registration applications during this reporting period. 4,523 of these were processed within three days of receipt, resulting in a success rate of 99.5%
(4,523/4,547).
Factors Affecting Results
Virtually every individual license and business registration is processed online and completed immediately. Fewer than 100 paper applications are received each year and, unless some information is
missing or is incorrect, are processed within 48 hours of receipt.

How Are We Doing
381 individuals took the Board's tax preparer licensing exam during this reporting period. 304 passed the exam, resulting in an overall pass rate of 79.8% (304/381).
Factors Affecting Results
Two factors have helped to increase the pass rate on this exam. One is an extensive review and editing of every exam question by the Board. Questions that were deemed to be confusing, poorly
written, or no longer relevant were removed or re-written. The second factor is the outsourcing of the exam administration to PSI exams. PSI's professional exam reviewers help to ensure the efficacy
of the Board's exam questions.
