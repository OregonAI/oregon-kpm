How Are We Doing
For reporting year 2019, the board has resolved approximately 10% of cases withing 180 days of receipt. While a decrease from last year and not yet up to the target, this still represents an
increase over the past several years. What is not clear from this metric is that the board was able to adjudicate more cases during this time period than in past years and is on target to clear even
more in the current period. The board is continuing to refine its compliance process to meet the target.
Factors Affecting Results
The number of complaints that the board can resolve within 180 days is affected by a number of factors. First, the complexity of the cases received by the board. Second, the board has had no
vacancies, which decreases the likelihood that meetingis will have to be cancelled for lack of a quorum. Two board positions will need to be filled in the upcoming year. It is critical to the board's
ability to resolve cases that those positions are filled. Third, the board has modified its protocol for addressing cases to allow, where appropriate, for earlier action. Finally the board has hired a
temporary investigator to allow staff to focus attention on more urgent cases involving the possibility of client or other harm.

How Are We Doing
All licensees are required to complete continuing education as a prerequisite for renewal. The renewal process occurs during the licensee's birth month and required licensees to indicate the hours
of continuing education they have completed.
Factors Affecting Results
Each monthly renewal cycle, 20% of renewing licensees are randomly selected for audit of the continuing education hours they have submitted. These renewing licensees must submit to our office
evidence demonstrating completion of all required continuing education. Licenses are not renewed until licensees have completed and submitted evidence of having completed the required
continuing education.
