How Are We Doing
LC's goal is to provide premium legal and publication services to the Legislative Assembly. In August 2020, a customer satisfaction survey was conducted to rate the overall service of the agency. The
survey was sent to Legislators, legislative staff and the lobby. There were 89 respondents who rated the Legislative Counsel section of the survey. The percentage of respondents who rated Customer
Service as "Good" or "Excellent" for Timeliness - 93%; Accuracy - 85%; Helpfulness - 93%; Expertise - 96%; Overall Quality of Services - 90%. Ninety-five percent of respondents answered that the
Quality of Services Provided by Legislative Counsel were "Getting Better" or "Staying the Same".
