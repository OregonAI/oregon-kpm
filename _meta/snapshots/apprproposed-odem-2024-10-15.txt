How Are We Doing
Multiple factors have resulted in ODEM's decision to delay seeking enhanced status until 2026. More specifically, FEMA took over two years to award the grant (BRIC FY2021) to update the state’s
natural hazard mitigation plan posing significant challenges in meeting the additional requirements for enhanced status before September 2025 plan expiration. If allowed to expire we would be
ineligible for millions in Federal funds. As such, ODEM will proceed with the scope of work change from enhanced to a standard plan update to avoid expiration.  ODEM will request FEMA conduct a
mid-plan review for enhanced status in January of 2026.
